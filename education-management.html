Overview

EduManage is an education management platform built to organize the daily academic and administrative workflow of an educational institution in one place. The system provides dedicated areas for students, teachers, fees, examinations, attendance, classes and administration.

Core functionality

Dashboard

Central overview of academic and administrative activity.

Students

Manage student records and academic information.

Teachers

Organize teacher records and responsibilities.

Fees

Track fee records and payment information.

Exams & Marks

Manage examinations, marks and academic results.

Attendance

Record and monitor attendance information.

Classes

Organize classes and academic groups.

Timetable

Manage schedules and class timings.

Notices

Publish and organize important announcements.

Expenses

Keep track of institutional expenses.

Settings

Configure system preferences and controls.

Admin Panel

Central administration and system management.

Project highlights

  • • Centralized academic and administrative management.
  • • Separate modules for students, teachers and examinations.
  • • Fee, attendance, timetable, notice and expense tracking.
  • • Admin-focused controls for managing the overall platform.
